# Black Rock Data

> **Data. Insight. Possibility.**

Black Rock Data is a Kenyan data analytics and research services company founded by **Rebecca Rose** and **Brandon Manase**.

We help **individuals, researchers, businesses, and organizations** turn raw, complex, or disorganized data into meaningful insights that support research, improve performance, and inform better decisions.

Our work brings together **data analytics, statistical methods, research support, business intelligence, data visualization, and data management** to provide practical and accessible data solutions.

## Our Approach

We believe data should be more than numbers sitting in spreadsheets or databases.

Our work focuses on moving from:

**Raw Data → Organized Data → Analysis → Insight → Better Decisions**

We prioritize:

* **Accuracy** — using reliable data and appropriate analytical methods.
* **Clarity** — making analytical findings understandable and useful.
* **Practicality** — solving real research and business problems.
* **Accessibility** — making professional data expertise available to individuals as well as organizations.
* **Integrity** — handling data, research, and client information responsibly.
